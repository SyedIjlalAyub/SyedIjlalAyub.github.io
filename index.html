01 / NUMBER LOGIC

Everything in its place.

Fill the board with 1–9. Every row, every column, and every 3 × 3 box must contain each number exactly once.

  1. Choose an empty cell and check its row, column, and box.
  2. Use the number pad to enter a value, or Notes to keep track of possibilities.
  3. Keep narrowing the choices until the whole board is complete.

A place to start Look for a row, column, or box with just one number missing.

02 / PICTURE LOGIC

Let the clues take shape.

LogiGrid is a nonogram. The numbers beside each row and column describe groups of consecutive filled cells, in order.

  1. Read the clues from left to right, or top to bottom.
  2. Leave at least one empty cell between separate groups.
  3. Use rows and columns together to decide which cells to fill and which to mark empty.

A place to start Look for a line whose groups and required gaps fill its entire length.

03 / WORD LOGIC

A thought, waiting to be found.

A quote is hidden behind a letter substitution. Work out which real letter each cipher letter represents to uncover the words.

  1. Look for short words and repeated letter patterns.
  2. Select a cipher letter and enter your guess. Its matching letters share that guess.
  3. Use the emerging words to refine your guesses and reveal the full quote.

A place to start A one-letter word in English is usually “A” or “I.” Check how that guess fits elsewhere.

04 / SPATIAL LOGIC

The next move changes everything.

Reach the target by activating numbered tiles. Each tile fills itself and the next N unfilled cells in the direction you choose.

  1. Select a numbered tile, then choose a direction.
  2. Already-filled cells are skipped and do not use up the tile’s count.
  3. Choose the order of your moves so a path can reach the target.

A place to start Work backwards from the target. Which cells need to be filled first so your final tile can reach it?
