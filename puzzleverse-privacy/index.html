PuzzleVerse

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

PuzzleVerse is an Android puzzle game developed by Syed Ijlal Ayub. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app, its advertising and cloud-save features, or contact the developer.

Android package: com.syedijlalayub.puzzleverse
Privacy contact: syedijlalayub@gmail.com

1. Information stored on your device

PuzzleVerse stores puzzle progress, moves, elapsed time, hints, results, game history, statistics, achievements, streaks, token balances and settings on your device. It also stores information needed to manage advertising choices, ad frequency and earned rewards. These records let you resume puzzles, retain progress and prevent duplicate rewards.

Android backup or device transfer may include app data, depending on your device settings. These backups are separate from Play Games cloud saves.

2. Google Play Games and cloud saves

PuzzleVerse uses Google Play Games Services for player identification and cloud saving. When connected, the app uses your Play Games player identifier and display name to associate saved progress with the correct player. Game progress and related records are sent to Google's Saved Games service so they can be restored or synchronized.

Google handles Google-account authentication; PuzzleVerse does not receive your Google-account password. Google processes information under its own privacy policy. Cloud features require a connection and an available Play Games account.

3. Advertising and rewarded ads

PuzzleVerse uses Google AdMob to display occasional ads after successful puzzles and optional rewarded ads for additional hints or continuing an eligible failed puzzle. A reward is granted when the advertising SDK confirms it was earned.

Google's advertising SDK can collect and share the following information for ad delivery, measurement, analytics, diagnostics and fraud prevention:

  • IP address, which can be used to estimate approximate location.
  • Device and account-related identifiers, including advertising ID and app set ID where available.
  • App and advertising interactions, such as launches, taps and video views.
  • Technical and diagnostic information about app and SDK performance.

Actual processing depends on your privacy choices, device settings, region and Google's ad-serving configuration. PuzzleVerse does not request precise GPS location. Advertising can involve Google and advertising partners identified in the applicable consent message.

Read Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps and sites that use its services.

4. Advertising privacy choices

PuzzleVerse uses Google's User Messaging Platform to present privacy messages where applicable and record your choices. Where available, open Settings → Advertising privacy choices to review or change those choices, including withdrawing consent or using an available opt-out.

You can also manage your advertising ID in Android settings. Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily remove all ads; limited or non-personalized ads may still be available under the applicable settings and rules.

5. Contacting the developer

If you email the developer, your email address, message and any information you choose to attach are used to respond to your enquiry, provide support or address your privacy request. Please do not send passwords or other unnecessary sensitive information.

6. Use and disclosure

Information is used to operate and restore the game, keep player saves separate, deliver and measure advertising, honor privacy choices, administer rewards, prevent abuse and respond to support requests. Third-party services process information as described above. Information may also be disclosed where necessary to meet a legal obligation or protect users and the service.

Google and other service providers may process information in countries other than where you live, under their published privacy practices and applicable safeguards.

7. Retention and deletion

Local game records remain until removed through device controls or otherwise replaced or deleted by the app. To remove local data, use Android's app settings for PuzzleVerse and select the storage option to clear app data. Uninstalling also normally removes local app data. Backups or cloud saves can restore previously saved information.

Clearing local storage does not by itself delete Play Games data or Android backups. To remove Play Games records, use Google's instructions for deleting data for a specific game. Manage device backups separately in your device or Google-account settings. If you want a fresh start, address both local data and cloud copies; reconnecting an existing local save may upload it again.

Google retains information it processes according to its own policies and your account settings. Support correspondence is retained while needed to resolve the enquiry and for any applicable legal requirements. For requests concerning information held by the developer, email syedijlalayub@gmail.com. The developer cannot directly erase Google's independently held advertising or account records.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of personal information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or opt out of certain advertising uses. Contact the developer to exercise rights concerning information they hold. For information managed by Google, use Google's privacy and account controls. You may also contact your local data-protection authority.

9. Children and privacy

PuzzleVerse does not ask players to submit their age, contact details or a personal profile directly to the developer to play puzzles. Third-party services may nevertheless process information as described in this policy. If you are a parent or guardian with a concern about a child's information, contact the developer so the concern can be investigated and appropriate steps taken.

10. Security

PuzzleVerse uses Android app storage protections and encrypted network connections for its integrated online services. No method of storage or transmission provides an absolute security guarantee.

11. This policy page

This page contains no advertising, analytics scripts or contact form. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

12. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app or its data practices change. The revised version will be published here with an updated date. Where required, additional notice or consent will be provided.
